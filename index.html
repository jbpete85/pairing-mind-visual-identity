Archivo and Manrope extend the Typewolf research within the free-only constraint. Bricolage is an additional candidate from its designer’s open-source project. All three, plus the supporting Source Sans 3, are supplied under the SIL Open Font License; notices are included in the fonts folder.

Headlines vary by direction. Source Sans 3 stays constant for body text so the comparison stays readable. Editable Google Slides font availability still needs checking before a production choice.

Sample compositions and copy only. Current-logo mode preserves the existing Dock A geometry and wordmark, with palette-adapted colors. The new logos are unapproved graphic studies. The project preview is illustrative; it is not a student result or testimonial. No live brand assets have changed.
